DARTBOARD MARKER PLACEMENT GUIDE
========================================

Overview:
This guide explains how to place markers on your dartboard
for computer vision detection and calibration.

Required Materials:
- 4 ArUco markers (or placeholder descriptions)
- Dartboard with clear corner access
- Tape or adhesive for marker placement
- Measuring tape for consistent positioning

Placement Instructions:
1. Prepare your dartboard - ensure it's mounted securely
2. Measure 2-3 inches from each corner of the dartboard
3. Place markers according to the following scheme:

   TOP-LEFT (Marker 0):
   - Position: Upper-left corner area
   - Distance: 2 inches from top and left edges
   - Orientation: Upright, readable from front

   TOP-RIGHT (Marker 1):
   - Position: Upper-right corner area
   - Distance: 2 inches from top and right edges
   - Orientation: Upright, readable from front

   BOTTOM-LEFT (Marker 2):
   - Position: Lower-left corner area
   - Distance: 2 inches from bottom and left edges
   - Orientation: Upright, readable from front

   BOTTOM-RIGHT (Marker 3):
   - Position: Lower-right corner area
   - Distance: 2 inches from bottom and right edges
   - Orientation: Upright, readable from front

Testing:
1. Use the detection system to verify marker visibility
2. Check that all 4 markers are detected consistently
3. Adjust lighting if detection is unreliable
4. Test different camera angles for optimal results

Troubleshooting:
- Markers not detected: Check lighting and camera angle
- Inconsistent detection: Ensure markers are flat and secure
- Poor calibration: Verify marker positions and distances
